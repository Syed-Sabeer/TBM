Our story

We started because nobody would quote us a straight price

TBM opened in 2013 with one container of cotton totes and a spreadsheet. The founders had spent years on the buying side of the promotional industry, chasing suppliers who published a price list and then negotiated a different one behind it.

So we built the opposite. No public price. One rate card per account, agreed up front, applied consistently to every person who logs in from that company — whether it's the owner placing a container order or a junior buyer topping up 250 pieces on a Tuesday.

Thirteen years later we hold stock on both coasts, run our own decoration floor, and still answer the phone on the second ring.
