How it runs

Four steps, and you sign off on two of them

Nothing goes to the floor without your written approval, and nothing leaves the floor without a first-off sample checked against it.

01 — ART

Send your files

Vector (AI, EPS, PDF) or 300dpi raster. Our studio cleans up, traces and separates at no charge — even if the file you inherited is a JPEG off a business card.

02 — PROOF

Approve the proof

Within 24 hours you get placement, size and Pantone match rendered on the actual body colour you ordered. Revisions are free and unlimited.

03 — RUN

Production

Your goods are pulled from stock, decorated and re-packed. A first-off sample is checked against the approved proof before the line continues.

04 — QC

Inspection and pack

Inspected to AQL 2.5, counted, polybagged and cartoned. Photos of the finished run are emailed with the shipment notice.
